**Short description (for the listing card):**
MintMate Pass unlocks mainnet execution in MintMate, the mint terminal that checks before it mints. 200 Founding passes free, 1 per wallet. 1,000 total on Base.

**Long description:**
MintMate is a mint terminal built around one rule: check before you mint. It runs a safety check on every drop, refuses to mint from fake or hollow records, and your keys never leave your browser vault.

The terminal proved itself first. On Nov 10 it ran its first clean mainnet mint through the repaired terminal: Pixel Foxes on Base, tx 0x3d3f8c3f667cbcf51f2c2c18577f1915949ec580232ee906551ff20db66b728c.

Now it has a pass. MintMate Pass is an ERC-721 on Base, hard capped at 1,000. The first 200 are Founding passes: free (gas only), allowlist, 1 per wallet, with a Founding trait so they stand out on secondary. After Founding closes, the public round opens at 0.005 ETH, max 3 per wallet. Royalty is 5%.

Checking stays free forever. The pass unlocks execution: arming mainnet mint tasks, preparing mint transactions, broadcasting through the terminal relay, copy-mint preparation, and fund moves. Founding claims run through MintMate itself, so the first 200 users experience the exact product they are unlocking.

Contract (verified): 0xe02Eca3160260D3f19A74f304D4a82cDd6758e1c
